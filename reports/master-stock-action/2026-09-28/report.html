06Methodology & reliability
Sell-through by depth: witnessed sell-outs cross-referenced with current discount, 21 days Prices: each product's latest snapshot within the last 8 days (not a single fixed date) Discount depth: uses first-observed price (not brand's compare-at, which brands inflate) Confidence intervals: approximate 95% binomial CI — within-brand correlation means true uncertainty is wider Speed to sell: median days from launch to first witnessed sell-out Age × discount: all categories combined — patterns may differ within subcategories Market temperature: last 4 complete calendar weeks, current in-progress week excluded All findings are observed associations, not causal estimates Excludes DeFacto sell-outs (stock unreliable), Tree, Dalydress (phantom data) Excludes sweatshirts/hoodies mistagged as "shirts" by a known classifier bug (~15% of the category) — fix pending a taxonomy backfill
