07Methodology & reliability
Confidence levels: based on brand count (12+ = high), product count (500+ = high), sell-out sample (200+ = high) Bestseller persistence: avg weeks each product appeared on bestseller lists out of 8 weeks of daily snapshots from 19 Shopify brands Demand index: sell-outs + products still out of stock — partial proxy for unmet demand, not a unit count Confidence intervals: approximate 95% binomial CI — within-brand correlation means true uncertainty is wider Speed to sell: median days from product launch to first witnessed sell-out — truncated for products under 21 days old Prices: each product's latest snapshot within the last 8 days (not a single fixed date) All price-band findings are observed associations — premium brands may sell more because of brand strength, not price Excludes DeFacto (stock unreliable), Tree, Dalydress. Under-tagged: Carina 17%, Mlameh 33%, Activ 40% Excludes sweatshirts/hoodies mistagged as "shirts" by a known classifier bug (~15% of the category) — fix pending a taxonomy backfill
