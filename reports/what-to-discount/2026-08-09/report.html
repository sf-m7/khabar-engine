02Does discounting actually clear the category?
% of sell-outs that happened while on discount — higher = discounting moves it

Read with care: a LOW value can mean discounting isn’t working or the category sells at full price without needing one. Cross-check distress — low-clear + high-distress is the true “discounting isn’t rescuing this” signal.
